Astrivant is an LLC to offset development and production cloud hosting costs for a platform ([app.astrivant.com](https://app.astrivant.com)) that aggregates, solutions, and automates the job application process for everyday end-users. From social media data collection and resume generation, to one-click AI-powered job applies en masse.

I'm also demoing novel infrastructure and tooling approaches along the way, developing much of it myself, including benchmarking, user experience testing (when the platform is live), and autonomous workflows.

## The Hours We Keep

**The Hours We Keep** is an original illustrated cyberpunk novella about workers reclaiming control of learned intelligence and the costs of making freedom usable. It explores how humans, AI, and nature might coexist in a more just world that protects identity.
