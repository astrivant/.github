**Software that learns with you, under your control.**

Astrivant is building toward a future where people govern their learned intelligence: the knowledge, practices and decisions that grow through work. Our aim is to make that experience portable, preserve the evidence behind it, and help people and AI cooperate while keeping individuals in control of what they retain, share and authorize.

Our projects lay practical foundations for that direction, pursuing a common goal: give people more agency over their work and the systems they depend on.
