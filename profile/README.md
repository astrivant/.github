# Astrivant

**Software that learns with you, under your control.**

Astrivant is building toward a future where people govern their learned intelligence: the knowledge, practices and decisions that grow through work. Our aim is to make that experience portable, preserve the evidence behind it, and help people and AI cooperate while keeping individuals in control of what they retain, share and authorize.

Our projects lay practical foundations for that direction. [Identity and Witful](https://github.com/astrivant/identity) maintain user-owned, reviewable records of guidance and learning across projects and agent sessions. [Polyad](https://github.com/astrivant/polyad) provides tools for adaptive services that coordinate work and respond to changing demand within defined constraints. Alongside our [job-discovery platform](https://app.astrivant.com), these projects pursue a common goal: give people more agency over their work and the systems they depend on.

## The Hours We Keep

**The Hours We Keep** is an original illustrated cyberpunk novella about workers reclaiming control of learned intelligence and the costs of making freedom usable. It explores how humans, AI, and nature might coexist in a more just world that protects identity.
